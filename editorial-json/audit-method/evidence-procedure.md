# Evidence procedure — adapted from audit_order.md

## Identity, coverage, and comparison

Begin with the exact exam/publication and the written content it actually contains. Map sections, examples, question numbering, source pages, and gaps before comparing wording. For PDFs, record one-based physical pages separately from printed folios. For other document types, use stable item, paragraph, table, or region locators rather than invented PDF pagination.

The [reference registry](../user_approved_reference_scans.md) supplies exact user-approved file/page boundaries. Approval applies to the unchanged full source and specified pages. It does not transfer to an overlay, excluded insert, duplicate with unverified contents, or a page-selected derivative. Neither a hash nor scan-like appearance authenticates custody. A rejected retype can still provide secondary transcription evidence without becoming approved style evidence.

Weigh identity, coverage, legibility, fidelity, independence, internal consistency, and answer logic per item. Do not vote by copies. The PDF and its OCR, imported JSON and its source HTML, or an original page and branded overlay are one witness for their shared reading. When ancestry is unknown, say so.

Separate what the source visibly contains, what its provenance establishes, and why the editorial chooses or departs from it. High confidence in a visible comparison is compatible with lower confidence in the historical reconstruction. A previous editorial judgment or low uncertainty score cannot establish literal print. Unreadable source text remains null; absence requires inspected coverage, not a failed OCR/search hit.

## Focused resolution

Locate the stable item and field, state the strongest alternatives, and confirm alignment. Inspect the relevant source span and its continuation alongside all options and related notes. Check current JSON before alleging that something is missing. Use other useful indexed witnesses where they could discriminate; record both actual attempts and useful exclusions.

Test Japanese grammar, lexical meaning, discourse, reference links, and task intent independently of the inherited key. A repair should explain why an awkward distractor is an accidental defect rather than the intended wrong answer. Exact parallels can help; broad analogy may establish only plausibility. Focused external research can supplement local evidence, but its scope and ancestry must be disclosed.

Choose a reading when it has a defensible evidential advantage. High confidence needs a strong item-specific basis without a material unexplained rival. A medium-confidence conjecture may be justified even without a witness containing its exact proposed wording, but it needs a public provisional-fix record. If neither alternative gains enough support, retain a provisional value or report the gap. Do not contact exam addresses or infer historical contact details from present domain ownership.

## Operational decision table

| Trigger | Treatment and limits |
|---|---|
| Missing or corrupted written instruction | Use a verified same-level/task scaffold when justified. Record extrapolation; a command pattern does not supply a whole worked example. |
| Kana/kanji, names, contractions, or spelling variants | Start with the witnessed expression and its function, author, genre, and period. Do not normalize everything to a preferred spelling or presumed difficulty. |
| Awkward grammar or apparently wrong options | Read the complete task and all choices. Preserve intentional distractors; fluency alone does not prove corruption. |
| Ruby or underline differences | Inspect exact base spans, readings, repetitions, endpoints, and adjacent reference numbers. A plain-text excerpt may hide markup already present. |
| STAR questions and fragment boundaries | Verify all four fragments, slots, star position, worked sequence, and answer mapping. The same assembled sentence can arise from different boundaries. |
| Tables, spatial wording, notes, numbers, or missing content | Preserve meaningful rows, units, conditions, reference links, and printed spatial wording. Do not invent arbitrary names, dates, or contact details from naturalness. |
| Mixed approved/unapproved pages or conflicting witnesses | Apply exact page scopes first, then legibility, ancestry, context, and coverage. Approval does not fill absent pages. |
| A stale or false recorded difference | Recompare the live typed value with the exact source. Retire a false difference while preserving history; this does not automatically close a public case. |

The [style digest](reference-style.md) illustrates these limits. These rules support item-specific investigation; they are not a global replacement list. Record concise reasons, evidence, alternatives, and remaining uncertainty. Validation can check consistency of those records, not whether a source was actually inspected correctly.
