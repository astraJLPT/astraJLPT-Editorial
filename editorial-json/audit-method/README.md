# Adapted audit guide

This is a release-facing adaptation of the workspace prompt `preprocess/readme.md` and its supporting contracts. It explains the method used to build the collection and supplies a starting guide for a deliberately scoped continuation. It removes completed-run launch instructions, old queue cursors, workstation paths, and unavailable workflow links. It does **not** claim that the original audits ran on schema v4 or that reading this guide starts a new audit.

The original working copies remain outside this repository. [Adaptation provenance](adaptation-provenance.json) records their filenames and SHA-256 values. The current release is written-only, uses schema 4.0.0, and has the canonical IDs in [the alias map](../unit_aliases.json). Older H16–H28, v2/v3, and listening references in archived records describe their own historical stages.

Read this guide with the [evidence procedure](evidence-procedure.md), [record contract](record-contract.md), [principal-difference contract](principal-differences.md), [assessment methods](assessment-methods.md), and [reference registry](../user_approved_reference_scans.md). [Reference style notes](reference-style.md) summarize limited observations rather than universal spelling rules.

## Audit brief

Reconstruct the most probable vocabulary, grammar, and reading content represented by the selected exam or publication, using available sources and Japanese-language knowledge. Finish difficult evidence questions for a unit before advancing. Every PDF, OCR result, key, previous transcription, and prior decision can contain errors. Preserve intentional wording and meaningful formatting. Depart from print for a documented, item-specific reason; do not polish plausible variants into a house style. Keep observations, inference, and editorial choices distinguishable.

1. **Establish scope and baseline.** Identify the current unit, its aliases, exam generation, publication identity, written components, and known gaps. Preserve the current editorial, sidecar, public records, and their hashes before editing. Read its archived audit and relevant fixlog entries. A previous completed or terminal result is historical evidence, not a request to redo the unit.
2. **Locate the sources.** Use the per-unit index and selected entries from the global index. Resolve complete paths and exact hashes; short candidate names are not global identities. `src/` belongs to the external source collection. Repeated copies or a PDF and its OCR do not become independent witnesses. Exclude the private `zdiamond_scans` convenience folder, including linked paths.
3. **Check authority and identity.** Consult the registry for exact approved pages and exclusions. Workbooks and samples are publications, not automatically full sittings. Older Levels 1–4 are not asserted equivalents of modern N1–N5. A principal is the chosen comparison reference, not a verdict on every item.
4. **Inspect the written content.** OCR and text extraction aid navigation. Inspect every in-scope original page or content region, including examples, notes, diagrams, instructions, underlines, ruby, and continuations. Record actual coverage. For an approved primary source, consult additional witnesses to investigate concrete gaps or conflicts. Without approved coverage, compare a materially distinct witness across sections when available; document when none exists.
5. **Resolve each item in context.** Read instructions, all necessary passage material, the stem, every option, the answer mapping, and explanations. Test grammar, discourse, task intent, and the strongest rival reading. Intentional wrong answers remain wrong answers. Match answer text before transferring a number from a reordered source. Use a supported repair when evidence gives it an advantage; record conjecture and missing content honestly.
6. **Record changes and uncertainty.** Archive the current compressed fixlog under `gitignored_docs`, then merge new exact before/after records into the unit's one maintained `.jsonl.gz`, preserving its historical entries. Follow the transactional merge checks in the record contract. Update public cases for materially uncertain fixes and gaps, and principal differences for meaningful current departures. Stable question, annotation, case, and difference IDs survive an identity migration. Public evidence must be understandable without access to private candidates or tool chatter.
7. **Verify the result and report limits.** Check schema/structure, source-derived counts, question order, context, answer mapping, meaningful formatting, and current public snapshots. Rebuild or compare the relevant outputs and update accepted bindings only after the intended changes are understood. Separate “investigation finished with uncertainty” from “important available checks remain undone.” Report incomplete coverage without inventing a complete paper.

## Continuing from this package

The old workspace validators and closed-run orchestration are not bundled as a runnable audit pipeline. Do not copy obsolete `preprocess/tools/...` commands from archived notes into a release workflow. Current structural schemas live under `pdf-render/schema/`; composition, reader projection, installed-file checks, and ruby replay have supported commands in [the renderer README](../../pdf-render/README.md) and [rubi-restore](../../rubi-restore/README.md).

Those checks cannot replace source inspection or establish that every meaningful departure was recorded. Conversely, an open public case does not establish that an entire investigation is unfinished. Existing source approvals and retained editorial decisions remain in force within their recorded scope; this adaptation neither expands approvals nor closes cases.
