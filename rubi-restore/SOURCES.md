# Datasets and downloads

I supplied a broad collection of possible predictors and reading resources. The agent tested what helped; the final scorer does not use every downloaded list. This page distinguishes **considered** (inventoried as a candidate), **tested** (used in an experiment or check), **retained** (a role survived in the final process), and **packaged** (bytes are actually in this repository). These labels can overlap.

For the research snapshot’s exact upstream filenames, URLs, revisions, and recorded hashes, see [source-catalog.json](datasets/source-catalog.json). Download from the linked upstream project or its indicated release. Live endpoints may now return different bytes. The supported offline replay uses the already frozen projections and decisions; downloading a newer dataset does not reproduce the historical training run.

The source catalog also retains recorded terms/documentation URLs. Related conversions often share upstream data: MEXT and an Anki grade grid, JEV and BCCWJ-derived measures, or a dictionary and its generated furigana are not independent evidence simply because they are separate files. Preserve each upstream edition and its terms when reacquiring it.

## What this package can reproduce

[`datasets/manifest.json`](datasets/manifest.json) pins the selected packaged research files. [`datasets/features/`](datasets/features/) stores the exact 58-column numeric projections aligned to the frozen ranked rows; the model, queues, allowances, contextual verdicts, and final selections are compressed where appropriate under their run/release IDs. Those derived resources are packaged even when their original input datasets are not.

This is sufficient for the supported offline score/selection replay described in [README.md](README.md). It is not a claim that every original acquisition or full training experiment is included. Old run filenames retain their historical unit IDs; [the alias map](../editorial-json/unit_aliases.json) connects them to current publication IDs without changing frozen hashes.
